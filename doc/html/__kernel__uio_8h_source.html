50  * Limits
51  *
52  * N.B.: UIO_MAXIOV must be no less than IOV_MAX from <sys/syslimits.h>
53  * which in turn must be no less than _XOPEN_IOV_MAX from <limits.h>. If

56  * sysconf(3) to find out the correct value, or else assume the worst
